## What Are the Main AARP Benefits for 2026?

AARP, known for its dedication to improving the quality of life for seniors, offers a variety of benefits to its members. These benefits are designed to address the diverse needs of individuals aged 50 and older, although membership is open to those as young as 18.

### Healthcare and Wellness

Healthcare is a critical concern for many AARP members. The organization provides access to a suite of health-related benefits, including:

- **Medicare Supplement Insurance:** AARP partners with UnitedHealthcare to offer Medicare Supplement plans that help cover costs not included in Original Medicare, such as copayments and deductibles.
- **Prescription Discounts:** Through the AARP Prescription Discount Program, members can save significantly on medications not covered by insurance.
- **Vision and Dental Care:** AARP offers members discounts on eye exams, glasses, and dental services, making routine care more affordable.

### Travel and Leisure

Traveling is a cherished activity for many retirees. AARP ensures its members can enjoy their travels with a range of discounts:

- **Car Rentals and Hotels:** Members can save on car rentals and accommodations, with discounts available from major rental agencies and hotel chains.
- **Cruise Deals:** Exclusive deals on cruises allow members to explore the world at reduced rates.
- **Vacation Packages:** AARP partners with travel companies to provide discounted vacation packages tailored to various interests and budgets.

### Financial and Legal Resources

Financial stability is essential for a secure retirement. AARP provides resources and tools to help members manage their finances effectively:

- **Retirement Planning Tools:** Access calculators and guides to help you plan your retirement savings and determine your retirement income needs.
- **Social Security Guidance:** Get insights on when to claim Social Security benefits and strategies to maximize your income.
- **Legal Services Network:** Members can receive discounts on legal services for estate planning, wills, and other legal needs.

### Community and Advocacy

AARP is a formidable advocate for senior issues, working to influence legislation and policies that affect older Americans. They also offer community-building opportunities:

- **Local Events and Workshops:** Participate in events that connect you with peers and provide learning opportunities.
- **Volunteer Opportunities:** Engage with your community through AARP's volunteer programs, which focus on a range of social issues, from hunger to fraud prevention.
- **Advocacy Efforts:** AARP's lobbying efforts aim to protect social security, improve healthcare access, and ensure fair treatment for older workers.

## Common Questions About AARP Benefits

### Is AARP membership worth it for someone under 50?

While AARP is primarily known for serving those over 50, membership is open to anyone 18 or older. Younger members can benefit from discounts and financial planning resources that support long-term financial wellness.

### How much does AARP membership cost?

As of 2026, AARP membership costs about $16 per year, with discounts available for multi-year memberships. This fee provides access to a wide range of benefits and resources.

### Can AARP help with finding healthcare providers?

Yes, AARP offers resources to assist members in finding healthcare providers that accept Medicare and offer quality care at competitive rates.

### What financial planning resources does AARP offer?

AARP provides retirement calculators, investment guidance, and Social Security strategies to help members plan for a secure financial future. They also offer webinars and articles on various financial topics.

### How does AARP advocate for seniors?

AARP advocates for seniors by lobbying on issues like healthcare reform, Social Security protection, and age discrimination in the workplace. They work at both the state and federal levels to influence policy changes that benefit older adults.
