## What Are the Core AARP Benefits in 2026?

### Healthcare Savings and Resources

Healthcare is a primary concern for many older adults, and AARP offers several benefits to ease this burden. Members can access discounts on prescription medications, vision care, and hearing aids. Additionally, AARP provides resources for understanding Medicare, including comparisons of Medicare Advantage plans and supplemental insurance options.

#### Prescription Discounts

AARP partners with several pharmacies to offer savings on prescription medications. Members can save up to 61% on FDA-approved medications. It's important to review the list of participating pharmacies, as savings can vary.

#### Vision and Hearing Aids

AARP's vision discounts include savings on eye exams, glasses, and contact lenses through various optical providers. For hearing aids, members receive discounts that can make these essential devices more affordable.

### Travel and Leisure Benefits

Travel is a cherished activity for many retirees, and AARP offers numerous travel-related discounts. These include savings on hotel stays, car rentals, and cruise bookings. Members can also access exclusive travel guides and planning resources.

#### Hotel and Car Rental Discounts

AARP members can enjoy discounts at major hotel chains and car rental companies, making it easier to plan affordable vacations. These discounts can range from 10% to 25%, depending on the provider and location.

#### Cruise Offers

For those who prefer cruising, AARP has partnerships with leading cruise lines, offering members special rates and onboard credits. It's advisable to book early to take advantage of these offers, as they are often subject to availability.

### Financial and Retirement Planning

AARP provides tools and resources to assist with financial planning and retirement preparation. This includes access to online retirement calculators, investment advice, and workshops on managing finances in retirement.

#### Retirement Calculators and Tools

Members can use AARP's retirement calculators to estimate retirement savings needs and assess the potential impact of various investment strategies. These tools are user-friendly and provide personalized insights based on individual financial situations.

#### Investment Advice and Workshops

AARP hosts regular workshops and webinars that cover topics such as Social Security, estate planning, and investment strategies. These educational sessions are invaluable for those seeking to enhance their financial literacy and make informed decisions.

### Everyday Discounts

Beyond healthcare and travel, AARP offers discounts on everyday expenses, including dining, shopping, and utilities. These savings can add up significantly over time, contributing to an improved quality of life.

#### Dining and Shopping Savings

Members can save at numerous restaurants and retailers, with discounts typically ranging from 10% to 15%. It's worthwhile to carry your AARP card, as these discounts are often available simply by presenting it at participating locations.

#### Utility and Service Discounts

AARP also partners with service providers to offer discounts on utilities and other essential services, helping members manage their monthly expenses more efficiently.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is AARP, and who can join?

AARP is a nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering Americans aged 50 and over. Anyone aged 50 and above can join, regardless of employment status or income.

### How much does an AARP membership cost?

AARP membership fees are affordable, typically ranging from $12 to $16 annually. Often, multi-year discounts are available, providing additional savings.

### Can AARP members save on Medicare plans?

Yes, AARP offers resources and tools to help members compare Medicare plans and find options that best meet their needs. However, it's important to verify details with official Medicare resources or consult a licensed advisor.

### Are there any AARP benefits for caregivers?

AARP provides resources and support for caregivers, including access to articles, guides, and community forums. These tools can help caregivers navigate the complexities of eldercare.

### How do I access AARP discounts?

AARP discounts can be accessed by presenting your membership card at participating locations or using a special promo code online. Always check the AARP website for the latest offers and participating partners.
